# LOOB logo concepts — round one

**Summary:** LOOB is a reading companion that pairs page reading with voice, ambient sound, OCR, and optional eye tracking.

**Audience:** Readers who want a quiet, supportive way to engage with a physical page; the mark will first live in a web app header, bookmark-like controls, an app icon, and a favicon.

**Brand adjectives:** quiet, attentive, warm, crafted, capable.

**Avoid:** generic school-blue edtech, a literal eye camera, and a stock open-book glyph.

**Direction:** An original one-colour combination mark: a compact symbol that works at favicon scale and a custom geometric LOOB wordmark. The supplied book icon is visual reference only, never traced or reproduced.

**Round-one concepts:**

1. **Fold** — An open spread whose deliberate central fold makes the pages feel held rather than floating.
2. **Page turn** — Two interlocking page corners create a continuous reading motion and a near-square app icon.
3. **Reading window** — A quiet frame made of two open page arcs, with a centered point of focus.

**Provisional palette for a chosen direction:** charcoal `#2D2D2D`, paper `#F4EEDB`, sage `#B7C691`.
