# LOOB logo usage

- **Symbol:** Reading Window — two page arcs hold one point of focus, representing a companion that stays with the reader.
- **Primary application lockup:** sage symbol `#B7C691` with paper wordmark `#F4EEDB` on charcoal `#2D2D2D`.
- **Clear space:** keep at least the height of the central focus dot around every edge of the symbol or lockup.
- **Minimum size:** use the horizontal lockup from 112px wide; below that, use the symbol only. The symbol is legible at 16px.
- **Do not:** add shadows, gradients, outlines, rotate it, recolour its individual page arcs, or use the horizontal lockup at favicon scale.
